Flagship Project

Aquarius

Marine Lines, Mumbai

Partner: Sejal Realty MahaRERA: P51900047328

Registered as Sejal Aquarius, this is a luxury tower being developed as a joint venture between Vistaar Apaar Developers and Sejal Realty at Navi Wadi, Dadiseth Agiyari Lane, Marine Lines. It offers 1, 2 and 3 bed sea-view residences with amenities like an infinity pool, a fully equipped gym, a yoga and zumba deck, a creche and a rooftop party zone.

Our Legacy

Completed by Our Promoters

Redevelopment projects delivered by our promoters in Mumbai, ahead of Vistaar Apaar Developers' own incorporation.

Vaikunt Dham

Goregaon West, Mumbai

Partner: Home Today Renovations India Pvt. Ltd.

Located at Jawahar Nagar, Village Pahadi, Goregaon West, within walking distance of Goregaon railway station, this project offers 1RK and 1BHK homes with granite kitchen platforms, vitrified flooring, a branded elevator and multilevel stack parking.

Padma Villa

Malad West, Mumbai

Developer: Vistaar Developers

A residential redevelopment on Ramchandra Lane, Malad West, delivered by our promoters under Vistaar Developers, with design by KSN Architects and Hingoo Architects.

Le Midas

Malad West, Mumbai

Developer: Tirupati Realty Developers

A residential and commercial redevelopment on Ramchandra Lane, Malad West, delivered by our promoters under Tirupati Realty Developers, with the same design team of KSN Architects and Hingoo Architects.
