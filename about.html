Founded by Mr. Manish Sharma, Vistaar Apaar Developers exists to breathe new life into Mumbai's ageing housing societies through transparent, high-quality redevelopment.

Who We Are

A Mumbai-Based Redevelopment Company With Deep Roots

Vistaar Apaar Developers is a Mumbai-based redevelopment company. We take ageing buildings and turn them into modern homes that people are proud to live in.

The company was set up with one goal in mind: give Mumbai's older housing societies a fresh start, through redevelopment that's sustainable, well-built and, most importantly, honest.

That's really what we do. We work closely with cooperative housing societies across Mumbai and the suburbs, designing homes that bring together good architecture, solid construction and lasting value.

Our Philosophy

More Than Replacing Structures. Preserving Aspirations.

To us, redevelopment isn't just about replacing an old structure. It's about honouring what a family has built over the years, and giving them a home that's ready for generations to come.

Our Legacy

Backed by Years of Promoter Experience

Vistaar Apaar Developers is a newly incorporated company, but the experience behind it isn't new at all. Our promoters have spent years working alongside established names like Sejal Group, Surbhi Group and Ramkrishna Group on redevelopment projects across Mumbai.

2010: Padma Villa

Malad West. One of the earliest redevelopment projects delivered by our promoters, and the starting point for the philosophy we still follow today.

2012: Le Midas

Malad West. Another step forward for our promoters, staying true to quality construction and long-term value.

Mayadevi Building, Goregaon West

A project underway at Jawahar Nagar, Village Pahadi, Goregaon West, offering 1RK and 1BHK homes in partnership with Home Today Renovations India Pvt. Ltd.

Aquarius, Marine Lines

A key milestone came through a joint venture with Sejal Realty. Registered as Sejal Aquarius and marketed as Passcode Sea & Believe, this luxury tower at Marine Lines is currently underway.

Today: Vistaar Apaar Developers Pvt. Ltd.

Together, these projects add up to roughly 2 lakh square feet of development and modern homes for hundreds of families, a legacy that now continues under the Vistaar Apaar name.
